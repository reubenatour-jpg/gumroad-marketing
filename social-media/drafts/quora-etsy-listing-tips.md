# Quora Answer Draft — Etsy Listing SEO Tips

**Target Question:** "What are the best ways to optimise Etsy listings for search?"

---

**Answer:**

Etsy's search algorithm (as of 2026) weighs four main factors: relevancy, recency, listing quality score, and shipping settings. Here's what actually moves the needle:

**1. Front-load your titles.** Put your most important keywords in the first 3-5 words. "Custom Dog Name Tag Personalised" beats "Beautiful Handmade Personalised Dog Name Tag" because Etsy reads left-to-right.

**2. Use all 13 tags — but don't repeat words.** Etsy treats each tag as independent. If tag 1 is "dog name tag" and tag 2 is "personalised tag", you've only covered 3 unique keywords across 2 tags. Instead use tags like: `engraved pet tag`, `custom id tag`, `cat collar charm`, etc.

**3. The "Ask AI" shortcut.** Writing 13 unique, non-overlapping tags takes time. I actually built a prompt pack that generates optimised Etsy titles, tags, and descriptions from just a product photo — it saves me ~20 mins per listing. ([link](https://reubenator4.gumroad.com/l/EtsyListingPrompts))

**4. Photos > everything.** A/B test your first image. Etsy's click-through rate is heavily driven by the thumbnail. Clean backgrounds, good lighting, and showing scale win every time.

**5. New listings get a boost.** Etsy gives fresh listings a short ranking bump. If something's not selling after 30 days, deactivate it, tweak the photos/title, and re-list.

Hope this helps someone starting out — it took me way too long to figure out the tag duplication mistake. 🧠
