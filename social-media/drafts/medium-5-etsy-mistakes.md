## 5 Etsy Listing Mistakes That Kill Sales (And How to Fix Them in 10 Minutes)

**Mistake #1: Copy-paste descriptions**
Every listing says the same thing. Etsy's algorithm notices. Fix: Write unique descriptions for each listing — use prompt templates to speed this up.

**Mistake #2: Ignoring search tags**
Most sellers use 3-5 generic tags. Etsy allows 13. Fix: Use all 13 with long-tail keywords buyers actually search for.

**Mistake #3: No alt text on images**
Alt text helps Etsy understand your photos. Without it, you're invisible in image search. Fix: 5 descriptive alt-text prompts can cover your entire shop.

**Mistake #4: Forgetting the "About" section**
Buyers want to know who they're buying from. A blank About page = lost trust. Fix: Use 2-3 prompts to write your shop story, mission, and process.

**Mistake #5: Treating every listing the same**
Your bestseller has a different job than a new listing. Fix: Use different prompts for different goals — some sell, some educate, some build trust.
