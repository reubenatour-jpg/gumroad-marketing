**Tweet 2**
The formula for a high-converting Etsy listing:

1. Hook (first 3 words matter most)
2. Benefits over features (buyers don't care about your process)
3. Social proof (even if it's speculative)
4. Clear CTA

Most sellers nail 1 of 4.

**Tweet 3**
The breakthrough? I stopped writing from scratch and started using structured prompts that force me to hit all 4 points every time.

Like this:
"Write an Etsy listing title for [product] targeting [audience]. Include emotional hook, 3 benefits, and a CTA. Max 140 chars."
